# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in origofs — reporting a bug, asking a question, sending a
patch — to be a good experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Things that make this project better:

- Being kind and assuming good faith, especially with people who are new.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback about the *work*.
- Owning mistakes, apologizing, and moving on.
- Staying focused on what's best for the project rather than for being right.

Things that are not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages — and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@danplischke](https://github.com/danplischke),
via GitHub. Reports will be handled discreetly, and the reporter's identity will
not be shared with the person reported.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that violate
this code, and may ban a contributor temporarily or permanently for behaviour they
consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
